Analysis definition and provenance

Hann periodograms: 2-second windows, no overlap, constant detrend, 0.5 Hz spacing. Power density is in digital full-scale squared per hertz. Candidate power is the mean density within ±1 Hz; local background is the median density 3–10 Hz from the peak. Each confirmation window is compared with the mean fan-off confirmation spectrum, not an arbitrarily aligned off window. Reported band power sums PSD bins over 35–350 Hz.

No per-recording scaling, waveform subtraction, model fitting, threshold retuning, or simulated ear transfer is applied. Criteria are an engineering screen, not a statistical significance test; there is only one sequential pair.
